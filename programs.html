Program 02 · Claims substantiated

Paid media

An objective claim has to be substantiated before it runs, so we ask for the basis of a performance, comparison, savings or health claim before writing it into an ad. If there is no basis we will not publish it, which occasionally means weaker copy than a competitor's. A claim that cannot be supported is a liability sitting in your ad account.

Program 03 · Consent, evidenced

Email and SMS

Marketing texts need prior express written consent and the statutory damages run per message, which is why this is such active class action territory. We will not send to a list whose consent provenance cannot be shown, and we will not touch purchased lists. On email: accurate sender, honest subject lines, and unsubscribes honoured promptly rather than eventually.

  • Businesses
  • Consent evidenced
  • No purchased lists

Program 04 · Including the flat months

Reporting

We also will not run a reporting narrative built on metrics we bought.
